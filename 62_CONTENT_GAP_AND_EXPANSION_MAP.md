# Content Gap and Expansion Map

## Strong Content Areas
- Verification of basic legal entity existence (Türkiye & Ukraine).
- General red flags and public records limitations.
- Logistics and coordination of physical visits and interpreters.

## High Priority Gaps
- **Market Entry & Orientation:** Strategic planning guides for new jurisdictions.
- **Logistics Coordination & Customs:** Non-legal overviews of how goods physically move across target borders.
- **Tools Expansion:** Additional categories like IP registries and Customs databases.

## Medium Priority Gaps
- **Sanctions & Compliance:** Overviews of what dual-use goods are and why professional clearance is critical. (Must strictly avoid giving legal advice).
- **Payment Safety:** Escrow, Letters of Credit, and secure payment best practices.

## Low Priority Gaps
- **Country Profiles:** General economic overviews.
- **News/Updates:** Not a priority for the beta phase; highly maintenance-intensive.
