# Verification and Trust Content Standard

This standard applies to all guides in the "Verification & Trust" category.

1. **The Registry Distinction:** Every guide must explicitly state that finding a company in a state registry proves legal existence, not commercial reliability, product quality, or financial health.
2. **Actionable Steps:** Guides must offer step-by-step procedures that an ordinary business user can actually execute (e.g., "Request the Tax Plate," "Check MERSİS").
3. **No False Tooling:** We do not claim to provide global verification engines, APIs, or automated due diligence checks. We point users to the official state tools or reputable third parties documented in the Tools Architecture.
4. **Red Flags Framework:** Red flags must be framed as risk indicators ("investigate further"), not automatic proof of fraud, avoiding sensationalism.
