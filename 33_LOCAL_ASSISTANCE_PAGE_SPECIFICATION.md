# Local Assistance Page Specification

## Purpose
Define pages pitching DealyVIP's coordination services. These must be informational, contact-oriented, and strictly bounded by realistic availability limitations.

## Recommended Structure

1. **Purpose:** Clearly state what DealyVIP can coordinate (e.g., "Business Interpreter Coordination in Istanbul").
2. **Typical Situations:** Why a user needs this (e.g., "Negotiating with a new supplier").
3. **Possible Assistance Areas:** Bullet points of specific tasks we can coordinate.
4. **Availability Limitations (Mandatory):** "Availability of physical coordination depends on exact location, current local conditions, and specific requirements."
5. **What Users Should Prepare Before Contacting:**
   - Exact objective.
   - Specific city/region.
   - Desired dates/timeline.
   - Specific language requirements.
6. **Related Guides:** Links to preparatory reading.
7. **Contact CTA:** "Contact us to discuss your requirements."

## Anti-Hallucination Rules
- Do not imply guaranteed immediate availability.
- Do not use e-commerce phrases ("Book now", "Add to cart").
- Do not imply DealyVIP employees directly perform licensed activities (e.g., "Our lawyers will..." -> "We coordinate with licensed local lawyers...").
