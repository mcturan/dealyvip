# Sprint 11 Source Evidence Record

## MERSİS (Central Registration System)
- **Organization:** Ministry of Trade (Ticaret Bakanlığı)
- **Purpose:** Central database for commercial entity registration.
- **Official URL:** `mersis.ticaret.gov.tr`
- **Evidence Status:** Verified
- **Access Notes:** Basic company search (by name, VKN, or MERSİS number) is publicly available. However, detailed company structure, representative lists, and historical records require authentication via e-Devlet (Turkish citizen ID).
- **Verification Method:** Direct Knowledge / Previous Sprint 9.1 Correction
- **Verification Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Content Pages:** How to Verify a Company, Verifying MERSİS Registration, Due Diligence

## Turkish Trade Registry Gazette (Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi)
- **Organization:** TOBB (Union of Chambers and Commodity Exchanges of Turkey)
- **Purpose:** Official publication of corporate registry changes.
- **Official URL:** `ticaretsicil.gov.tr`
- **Evidence Status:** Verified
- **Access Notes:** Searching the gazette index is possible, but viewing or downloading the official PDF documents requires creating a free user account. 
- **Verification Method:** Direct Knowledge
- **Verification Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Content Pages:** How to Verify a Company, Due Diligence

## GİB e-Archive Invoice Verification (e-Arşiv Fatura Sorgulama)
- **Organization:** Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı)
- **Purpose:** Verifying the issuance of an e-Archive invoice.
- **Official URL:** `ebelge.gib.gov.tr` / `efatura.gov.tr`
- **Evidence Status:** Verified
- **Access Notes:** Publicly accessible. Requires the vendor's Tax ID (VKN), the Invoice Number, and the Invoice UUID to confirm if the invoice was successfully processed by the GİB system.
- **Verification Method:** Direct Knowledge
- **Verification Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Content Pages:** How to Check a Turkish e-Invoice

## GİB Tax Plate Verification (e-Vergi Levhası Sorgulama)
- **Organization:** Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı)
- **Purpose:** Confirming active tax registration status.
- **Official URL:** `ivd.gib.gov.tr`
- **Evidence Status:** Verified
- **Access Notes:** Publicly accessible but requires exact parameters (e.g., VKN and the specific Tax Office name) to return a positive match.
- **Verification Method:** Direct Knowledge
- **Verification Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Content Pages:** Due Diligence, How to Verify a Company
