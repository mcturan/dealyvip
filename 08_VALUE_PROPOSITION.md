# Value Proposition

## Purpose
Define the customer value created by DealyVIP.

## Core Value Proposition
DealyVIP provides reliable information, verified local access, and practical coordination for cross-border business in select markets.

## Service Distinctions

### What DealyVIP Provides Directly
- Curated, human-readable, AI-accessible business information
- Initial practical assistance for market entry
- Basic local coordination and matching

### What DealyVIP Coordinates
- Connections to local service providers (e.g., interpreters, business concierges)
- Introductions to appropriate legal, accounting, tax, banking, immigration, logistics, and other specialists where genuinely available

### What Requires Licensed/Regulated Professionals
- Legal advice and company formation
- Tax and accounting advice
- Financial services and payment intermediation
- Official audits and certifications

### What Cannot Yet Be Promised
- Availability of any specific service in any target market until operational capability is verified (Status: VALIDATION REQUIRED).
- Guarantees of counterparties, visas, permits, bank accounts, investments, or commercial outcomes.
