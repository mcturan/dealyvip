# Content Quality Standard

## Core Philosophy
Every substantive guide on DealyVIP must exist to solve a real user problem, not merely to occupy a URL.

## Structure Requirements
Each guide should answer:
1. **What is the problem?** (The core risk or need)
2. **Who is this relevant to?** (The target audience context)
3. **What should the reader understand?** (The mechanism or law)
4. **What practical steps can they take?** (Actionable guidance)
5. **What should they verify?** (Specific tools or documents)
6. **What risks or limitations exist?** (Boundaries of the advice)
7. **What related topics should they read next?** (Semantic relations)

## Separation of Concern
- **FACT:** Must be traceable to a verifiable source.
- **GUIDANCE:** Best practices based on facts.
- **LIMITATION:** Explicit warnings about what cannot be guaranteed (e.g., "A clean registry check does not guarantee a company is not a shell entity").

No SEO filler. No keyword stuffing. No generic AI-style introductions.
