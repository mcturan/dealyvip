# Partner Requirements

## Purpose
Identify the specific categories of local partners required to deliver DealyVIP's coordinated services, establishing the baseline requirements before any partner is engaged.

## General Requirement
DealyVIP does not invent fictional partners. A partner is only considered "active" when they have passed verification and an agreement or clear working relationship is in place.

## Required Partner Categories

### 1. Field Agents / Inspectors
- **Purpose:** Conduct physical site visits, verify factory existence, document loading processes.
- **Requirement:** Reliable individuals with transportation, photography equipment, and local language fluency. Must pass background checks.

### 2. Legal / Corporate Law Firms
- **Purpose:** Handle company formation, immigration (visas/work permits), and regulatory compliance for clients.
- **Requirement:** Must hold valid local legal practice licenses. Must have bilingual (English + local language) capabilities.

### 3. Interpreters / Translators
- **Purpose:** Facilitate business meetings, trade fair visits, and document translation.
- **Requirement:** Proven fluency (certifications preferred). Must understand business/technical terminology.

### 4. Logistics Providers / Customs Brokers
- **Purpose:** Coordinate shipping, warehousing, and customs clearance.
- **Requirement:** Must hold required customs brokerage licenses and have a track record of international shipping.

### 5. Business Concierge / Local Transport
- **Purpose:** Airport pickups, safe local travel, hotel coordination.
- **Requirement:** Licensed commercial drivers, vetted vehicles, reliable scheduling.

### 6. Accounting / Tax Advisors
- **Purpose:** Advise clients on tax implications and bookkeeping.
- **Requirement:** Licensed local accountants/CPAs.
