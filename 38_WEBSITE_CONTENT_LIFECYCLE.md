# Website Content Lifecycle

## Purpose
Define a lightweight governance process for content moving from idea to publication and eventual retirement.

## Lifecycle Stages
1. **IDEA:** Topic identified from Search Intent Map or customer inquiry.
2. **RESEARCH:** Verification of facts (especially sanctions/visas) using authoritative sources.
3. **DRAFT:** Written in Markdown, adhering to the Problem Page Model.
4. **REVIEW:** Checked against the Country Content Model and Operational Capability Model for regulatory overreach.
5. **APPROVED:** Final sign-off by project owners.
6. **PUBLISHED:** Deployed to the website.
7. **PERIODIC REVIEW:** Time-sensitive pages (visas, sanctions) flagged for 3-6 month review cycles.
8. **UPDATE / RETIRE:** Content is refreshed or removed if the service/reality changes.

## Rules
- **Source Requirements:** Legal or regulatory facts must be linked to official sources.
- **Update Dates:** `Last Updated: [Date]` must be visibly changed upon content modification.
- **Language Synchronization:** If a critical English page (e.g., Sanctions) changes, linked translations must be flagged for sync to prevent legal liability.
- **Correction Process:** Errors found post-publication must be corrected in Git and pushed immediately.
