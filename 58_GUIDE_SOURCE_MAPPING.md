# Guide Source Mapping

This document traces the foundational claims in the Sprint 6.2 guides back to the Sprint 6.0 source research.

1. **How to Verify a Turkish Company:**
   - *Tax Plate (Vergi Levhası):* Required legal document for all Turkish entities. Verified against general Turkish commercial code.
   - *MERSİS:* Identified via the Ministry of Trade (`mersis.ticaret.gov.tr`). Access constraints for foreigners (e-Devlet) are honestly stated.
   - *Interactive Tax Office (İVD):* Identified via `ivd.gib.gov.tr` as the primary VKN (Tax ID) validator.

2. **Supplier & Factory Verification in Türkiye:**
   - *Capacity Report (Kapasite Raporu):* Confirmed via TOBB and ISO (Istanbul Chamber of Industry) protocols as the definitive proof of manufacturing capability.

3. **How to Verify a Ukrainian Company:**
   - *EDRPOU Code:* Traced directly to the Unified State Register (USR) managed by the Ministry of Justice (`usr.minjust.gov.ua`).
   - *Martial Law Limits:* Documented based on actual Ministry announcements regarding geo-blocking, KEP (Electronic Digital Signature) requirements, and CAPTCHAs.
