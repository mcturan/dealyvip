# Project Principles

## Purpose
Define non-negotiable principles that guide business, content, design, and technical decisions.

## Confirmed Principles
1. Trust is the primary product.
2. Simplicity before feature expansion.
3. Information before technology.
4. Human-readable, structured, and AI-accessible content.
5. Mobile-first user experience.
6. Static-first and low-maintenance implementation where practical.
7. No unsupported claims.
8. No invented facts or legal certainty.
9. Clear boundaries between assistance, coordination, and regulated professional services.
10. A visitor should reach a relevant contact path with minimal friction.
11. Prefer direct answers over generic marketing language.
12. Every material decision should be explainable and traceable.
