## Service Discovery Summary
A comprehensive set of candidate services was analyzed, spanning verification, matchmaking, logistics, legal coordination, and concierge support. These were strictly bounded by the requirement to refer regulated activities (legal, tax, banking) to licensed professionals. 

## Country Findings
- **Türkiye:** Highest immediate operational viability, though local field agents and registry access remain unverified.
- **Ukraine:** High physical risk for on-site services due to martial law. Remote verification is the most viable path.
- **Russia & Iran:** Extreme legal and sanctions risk (OFAC, FATF). Cross-border trade coordination and payment facilitation are effectively blocked. Remote verification of entities requires deep legal review.
