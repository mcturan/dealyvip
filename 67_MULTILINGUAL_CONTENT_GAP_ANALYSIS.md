# Multilingual Content Gap Analysis

## English (Baseline)
- Serves as the global fallback and structural foundation.

## Turkish Target
- **User Intent:** Turkish business people looking outward (e.g. trading with Ukraine) and looking to understand how to verify foreign entities.
- **Decision:** PRODUCE targeted guides on "Verify Ukrainian Company", "Verify Overseas Supplier", and "Factory Visit Prep". Do not produce Turkish guides about "How to verify a Turkish company" because native speakers already know or use different channels (e.g., e-Devlet directly).

## Ukrainian Target
- **User Intent:** Ukrainian businesses looking to evaluate Turkish manufacturers, verify Turkish companies, and arrange visits.
- **Decision:** PRODUCE guides on "Verify Turkish Company", "Turkish Factory Verification", and "Factory Visit Prep".

## Russian Target
- **User Intent:** Secondary audience.
- **Decision:** DO NOT PRODUCE YET. Ensure technical structure is ready, but keep it minimal to prevent scope expansion before verifying TR/UK success.
