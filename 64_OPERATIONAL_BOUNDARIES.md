# Operational Boundaries

The DealyVIP platform is built strictly on the following boundary rules.

## 1. Information vs. Legal Guarantee
- **Boundary:** We provide procedural information for verifying foreign entities.
- **Enforcement:** No guide will ever claim that an entity is "100% safe." All guides distinguish between a registry check and commercial due diligence.

## 2. Coordination vs. Execution
- **Boundary:** DealyVIP coordinates "observation visits" (eyes and ears). We do not perform "certified QA inspections."
- **Enforcement:** Replaced all instances of "we verify" or "our inspectors" with "we coordinate independent local professionals."

## 3. Financial Isolation
- **Boundary:** DealyVIP does not touch transaction capital.
- **Enforcement:** We do not offer escrow, letter of credit (LC) services, or act as a financial guarantor for international payments.

## 4. No Automated Magic
- **Boundary:** DealyVIP does not maintain an automated global company database.
- **Enforcement:** Tools pages explicitly route users to state-owned registries (e.g., MERSİS, USR) rather than wrapping them in a fake internal API.
