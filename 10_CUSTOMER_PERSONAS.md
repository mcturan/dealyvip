# Customer Personas

## Purpose
Define decision-relevant customer archetypes without pretending that they are statistically validated personas.

## Candidate Archetypes
- A foreign company seeking a Turkish supplier
- A Turkish company exploring Ukraine
- A business person needing local verification before payment
- An investor or entrepreneur needing coordinated entry support
- A visiting delegation needing practical local assistance

## Requirement
For each approved archetype define:
- objective,
- trigger,
- main uncertainty,
- decision criteria,
- preferred information,
- likely contact request.
