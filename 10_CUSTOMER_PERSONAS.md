# Customer Personas

## Purpose
Define decision-relevant customer archetypes without pretending that they are statistically validated personas.

## Approved Archetypes

### 1. Foreign Company Seeking a Local Supplier
- **Objective:** Find and engage a reliable manufacturing or trade partner.
- **Trigger:** Need to source materials/products from Türkiye, Ukraine, Russia, or Iran.
- **Main Uncertainty:** "Is this supplier real and capable of delivering?"
- **Decision Criteria:** Evidence of existence, clear communication, verified track record.
- **Preferred Information:** Supplier verification process, on-site visit options, business matching.
- **Likely Contact Request:** Request for supplier search or physical verification of a known prospect.

### 2. Company Exploring Market Entry
- **Objective:** Establish a business presence or evaluate market viability.
- **Trigger:** Strategic decision to expand into a target market.
- **Main Uncertainty:** "What are the legal, cultural, and practical barriers?"
- **Decision Criteria:** Clarity of information, access to vetted professionals.
- **Preferred Information:** Market overviews, coordination services, legal/tax boundaries.
- **Likely Contact Request:** Request for initial market consultation and coordination with local specialists.

### 3. Business Person Needing Pre-Payment Verification
- **Objective:** Prevent fraud before transferring funds.
- **Trigger:** Receiving an invoice from an unknown entity in a target market.
- **Main Uncertainty:** "Am I being scammed?"
- **Decision Criteria:** Speed and reliability of the verification service.
- **Preferred Information:** Company registration check, physical location verification.
- **Likely Contact Request:** Urgent request for basic or comprehensive company verification.

### 4. Visiting Delegation Needing Local Assistance
- **Objective:** Conduct successful in-person meetings and business travel.
- **Trigger:** Planning a business trip to a target market.
- **Main Uncertainty:** "How will we navigate language and logistical barriers?"
- **Decision Criteria:** Availability of bilingual support and local know-how.
- **Preferred Information:** Interpreter services, local transport coordination, concierge options.
- **Likely Contact Request:** Booking request for an interpreter or business guide.
