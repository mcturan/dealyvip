# Ukraine Business Research

## 1. Company Verification (Legal)
- **Primary Identifier:** The EDRPOU code (ЄДРПОУ) - an 8-digit unique identifier. It is the most reliable way to track a company, as names can change.
- **Official Registry (EDR / USR):**
  - **URL:** `usr.minjust.gov.ua`
  - **Managed by:** Ministry of Justice of Ukraine.
- **Secondary / Aggregator Platforms:**
  - **Opendatabot & YouControl:** Highly reputable Ukrainian tech platforms aggregating registry, tax, and court data. Often more accessible and user-friendly for due diligence than the raw government portal.

## 2. Time-Sensitive & Wartime Realities
- **Registry Access (TIME-SENSITIVE: As of 2024/2025):** Access to public registries has fluctuated during martial law to protect sensitive data. Some queries require an electronic digital signature (KEP/EDS) or are geo-blocked from outside Ukraine.
- **Travel & Logistics (TIME-SENSITIVE):** Airspace is closed. Business travel is exclusively via land borders (Poland, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Moldova).
- **Sanctions & Compliance:** Ukrainian entities strictly monitor counterparties for ties to Russia or Belarus. 

## 3. Operational Constraints
- **Document Verification:** Electronic document signing via Diia or third-party providers (Vchasno) is standard practice in Ukraine.
- **Legal Boundaries:** DealyVIP does not provide legal representation in Ukraine. We can explain the EDRPOU concept and point to official registries, but complex due diligence requires Ukrainian lawyers.
