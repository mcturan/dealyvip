# Sprint 6.0 Research Audit

## Research Scope
The research covered candidate topics for the first major content cluster:
1. Company Verification in Türkiye
2. Supplier Verification Before Buying from Türkiye
3. Factory Visit and Inspection in Türkiye
4. How to Start Doing Business in Ukraine
5. How to Verify a Ukrainian Company
6. Business Assistance Between Türkiye and Ukraine

## Sources Inspected
- Official Registries: MERSİS (`mersis.ticaret.gov.tr`), Ticaret Sicil Gazetesi, EDR/USR (`usr.minjust.gov.ua`), İnteraktif Vergi Dairesi.
- Reputable Secondary: Opendatabot, YouControl.

## Source Quality
- **High/Official:** All primary company verification steps are mapped directly to official state registries in Türkiye and Ukraine.

## Unsupported Claims Removed or Avoided
- Avoided claiming that MERSİS is universally accessible without a Turkish ID (e-Devlet barrier acknowledged).
- Avoided claiming DealyVIP provides licensed QC certificates or legal representation.
- Corrected outdated Ministry of Customs and Trade URL (`gtb.gov.tr`) to the current Ministry of Trade URL (`ticaret.gov.tr`).

## Time-Sensitive Areas
- Ukrainian airspace closures and logistics.
- Ukrainian registry availability and digital signature (KEP/EDS) requirements during martial law.
- These have been explicitly flagged in the Claim and Risk Register.

## Legal/Professional Boundary Risks
- The line between "coordinating an observation visit" (DealyVIP scope) and "executing accredited technical QC" (Requires licensed agency) is clearly drawn.
- The line between "explaining the EDRPOU registry" (Information) and "performing legal due diligence" (Requires Ukrainian lawyer) is established.

## Candidate Page Overlap
- Excessive overlap was found between "Company Verification in Türkiye" and "Supplier Verification". They have been structurally divided (Legal/Administrative vs. Commercial/Operational).
- "How to Start Doing Business in Ukraine" was deemed too broad and overlaps with verification topics.

## Research-Ready Pages (Sprint 6.1 Scope)
1. How to Verify a Turkish Company (Legal & Official Registries)
2. Supplier & Factory Verification in Türkiye (Operational Checks)
3. How to Verify a Ukrainian Company (EDRPOU & Registries)
4. Coordinating Business Assistance Between Türkiye and Ukraine

## Pages Requiring More Research
- None at this exact stage; the eliminated candidates were removed for scope/overlap reasons rather than lack of research.

## Critical Findings
- An independent review caught an outdated URL for MERSİS (`gtb.gov.tr` vs `ticaret.gov.tr`), which was corrected prior to this audit report.

## Open Questions
