# Navigation Model

## Purpose
Ensure the user can navigate the DealyVIP website simply and quickly, without being overwhelmed by a complex, portal-like mega-menu.

## Desktop Navigation
- **Top Header:** Clean, sticky navigation.
- **Maximum Top-Level Items:** 5 items.
  1. Countries
  2. Guides
  3. Local Assistance
  4. About Us
  5. Contact (Styled as a CTA button)
- **Dropdowns:** "Countries" and "Guides" may have simple, single-level dropdowns. No nested mega-menus.

## Mobile Navigation
- **Hamburger Menu:** Standard hidden menu pattern.
- **Priority:** The "Contact" CTA remains persistently visible (e.g., bottom sticky bar or prominent in the open menu).
- **Structure:** Expands to a single vertical list. Sub-menus expand accordion-style.

## Primary Navigation
The main header menu, designed to drive users toward their specific problem or country of interest.

## Secondary Navigation
- **In-page Sidebar (Optional for long guides):** A table of contents tracking scroll progress on heavy Guide pages.
- **Language Switcher:** Located in the top utility bar or primary menu, displaying only languages available for the *current* page context.

## Footer Navigation
Organized into 3-4 simple columns:
- **DealyVIP:** About Us, Contact
- **Explore:** Türkiye, Ukraine, Russia, Iran
- **Top Guides:** Verify a Company, Find a Supplier, Travel Prep
- **Legal:** Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer

## Breadcrumb Requirements
- Mandatory on all Guide, Country, and Assistance pages.
- Format: `Home > [Category] > [Page Name]`
- Enhances AI discoverability and user orientation deep in the site.

## Internal Linking Principles
- Contextual linking within text (e.g., linking from a "Company Setup" guide directly to a "Local Assistance" page).
- Avoid generic "Click here" links. Use semantic anchor text (e.g., "Read our guide on verifying a company in Türkiye").
