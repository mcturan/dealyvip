# Problem Page Model

## Purpose
Standardizes the structure for pages answering user questions (Problem Guides), ensuring they are highly AI-readable and directly useful.

## Standard Structure

1. **Direct Answer:** A 1-2 sentence immediate answer to the user's query, designed for AI snippets and quick reading.
2. **Short Summary:** Brief context on why this matters.
3. **Step-by-Step Guidance:** Clear, actionable steps the user can take.
4. **What You Can Verify (or Solve):** The realistic boundaries of the solution.
5. **What You Cannot Verify:** Honest limitations (e.g., "Registry data does not prove current financial health").
6. **Risks / Limitations:** Common pitfalls or regulatory constraints.
7. **When Local Assistance May Help:** A soft, relevant transition introducing DealyVIP's coordination services, if applicable.
8. **Related Guides:** Internal links to contextual topics.
9. **Last Updated:** Date of last content review.
10. **Sources:** Citations for legal/regulatory facts (if used).
