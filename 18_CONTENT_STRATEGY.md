# Content Strategy

## Purpose
Define the overarching strategy for content creation on the DealyVIP beta website, prioritizing high-intent user questions and AI discoverability over generic marketing material.

## Overall Content Strategy
The website operates on an **Information → Trust → Contact** model. The strategy is to intercept users actively searching for cross-border business solutions by providing direct, honest, and structured answers. We prioritize solving the user's immediate informational problem before inviting them to engage DealyVIP for local assistance.

## Target Users
- Foreign companies seeking local suppliers or market entry.
- Business professionals requiring verification before sending funds overseas.
- Visiting delegations needing logistical or interpretation support.
- Entrepreneurs needing orientation on company setup, banking, or visas.

## Content Types
1. **Country Guides:** High-level orientation for doing business in specific markets.
2. **Problem / Question Guides:** Direct answers to high-intent "How to" or "What is" queries (e.g., "How to verify a supplier").
3. **Practical Cross-Border Business Guides:** Process-oriented content to reduce risk (e.g., "What documents to request").
4. **Local Assistance Pages:** Descriptions of coordinated services, heavily bounded by realistic availability statements.

## Beta Priorities
The beta launch will focus strictly on Tier 1 pages (approx. 25-40 high-value pages) that answer the most urgent trust and verification questions, primarily centered around Türkiye and Ukraine, with heavily caveated informational guides for Russia and Iran. Artificial country symmetry will be avoided.

## Content Lifecycle
1. **Identify Intent:** Discover what questions users are asking.
2. **Draft Direct Answers:** Write concise, AI-readable responses.
3. **Review Boundaries:** Ensure no legal, tax, or guaranteed operational claims are made.
4. **Publish & Monitor:** Deploy content and monitor inquiry quality.
5. **Update:** Refresh content based on regulatory or operational changes (e.g., sanctions updates).
