# Sprint 6 Content Research Overview

## Research Objective
To establish a verified, legally and operationally accurate research foundation for the first DealyVIP content cluster covering business verification and coordination in Türkiye and Ukraine.

## Candidate Pages Evaluated
1. Company Verification in Türkiye
2. Supplier Verification Before Buying from Türkiye
3. Factory Visit and Inspection in Türkiye
4. How to Start Doing Business in Ukraine
5. How to Verify a Ukrainian Company
6. Business Assistance Between Türkiye and Ukraine

## Overlap Analysis
- **Company Verification in Türkiye** vs. **Supplier Verification Before Buying from Türkiye**: There is high overlap here. "Company Verification" is the legal process (MERSİS, Tax ID), while "Supplier Verification" includes evaluating production capacity, trade history, and fraud risks.
  - *Recommendation:* Keep them separate but cross-linked. One is legal/administrative, the other is operational/commercial.
- **Factory Visit and Inspection**: Distinct intent. A highly operational topic where DealyVIP's local coordination model is highly relevant.
- **How to Start Doing Business in Ukraine**: Too broad for a single page. It overlaps heavily with "How to Verify a Ukrainian Company".
  - *Recommendation:* Refocus to "Navigating the Ukrainian Business Environment (Wartime Context)" or strictly focus on the verification aspect first.

## Missing Content Opportunities
- **Verification Glossary / EDRPOU & MERSİS Explainer:** Dedicated pages explaining the core terminology.

## Strategic Rationale
DealyVIP must establish authority by providing exact, procedural answers to complex cross-border questions. Vague advice damages trust. Providing the actual URLs, code formats (e.g., EDRPOU), and limitations of official registries builds instant credibility.

## Recommended Final Content Cluster (Sprint 6.1)
1. **How to Verify a Turkish Company (Legal & Official Registries)**
2. **Supplier & Factory Verification in Türkiye (Operational Checks)**
3. **How to Verify a Ukrainian Company (EDRPOU & Registries)**
4. **Coordinating Business Assistance Between Türkiye and Ukraine**
