# Country Operational Analysis

## Purpose
Analyze the operational feasibility, required capabilities, and structural constraints for DealyVIP's candidate services across the four initial target markets.

## 1. Türkiye
### General Context
A major regional business hub. No comprehensive international sanctions. High potential for direct business matchmaking, logistics, and verification.
- **Language Capability Required:** Turkish, English
- **Local Access Required:** Field agents in major industrial hubs (Istanbul, Bursa, Izmir)
- **Risks & Constraints:** Currency volatility, changing regulatory environment for foreign business setup.

### Service Analysis (Türkiye)
- **Service:** Company Verification
  - **Delivery Model:** Direct/Coordination
  - **Required Capability:** Access to Turkish Trade Registry (Ticaret Sicil Gazetesi).
  - **Evidence:** NO VERIFIED EVIDENCE
  - **Risk:** Low
  - **Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED
  - **Next Validation Step:** Identify and test access to official registry databases or partner with a local B2B credit check agency.

- **Service:** Factory / Warehouse Visit
  - **Delivery Model:** Coordination
  - **Required Capability:** Vetted local field agents.
  - **Evidence:** NO VERIFIED EVIDENCE
  - **Risk:** Agent reliability, transport safety.
  - **Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED
  - **Next Validation Step:** Source and vet pilot field agents in Istanbul.

- **Service:** Legal & Company Setup Coordination
  - **Delivery Model:** Referral
  - **Required Capability:** Established relationship with a licensed Turkish corporate law firm.
  - **Evidence:** NO VERIFIED EVIDENCE
  - **Risk:** Liability for bad legal referrals.
  - **Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED
  - **Next Validation Step:** Interview and vet local legal partners.

## 2. Ukraine
### General Context
Operating under martial law due to ongoing conflict. Significant logistical and safety constraints.
- **Language Capability Required:** Ukrainian, English
- **Local Access Required:** Agents in relatively stable zones (e.g., Kyiv, Lviv).
- **Risks & Constraints:** High physical safety risks, disrupted logistics, martial law restrictions, travel advisories.

### Service Analysis (Ukraine)
- **Service:** Physical Address / Factory Visit
  - **Delivery Model:** Coordination
  - **Required Capability:** Local agents willing to travel within the country.
  - **Evidence:** NO VERIFIED EVIDENCE
  - **Risk:** Extreme physical safety risk, disrupted transport.
  - **Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED
  - **Next Validation Step:** Assess feasibility of maintaining a reliable agent network under martial law conditions.

- **Service:** Supplier Verification
  - **Delivery Model:** Direct/Coordination
  - **Required Capability:** Access to YouControl or official Ukrainian registries.
  - **Evidence:** NO VERIFIED EVIDENCE
  - **Risk:** High (factories may have relocated or suffered damage).
  - **Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED
  - **Next Validation Step:** Test registry access and verify operational status of suppliers remotely.

## 3. Russia
### General Context
Subject to comprehensive international sanctions (US, EU, UK, etc.). Cross-border banking and logistics are severely restricted or prohibited depending on the jurisdiction.
- **Language Capability Required:** Russian
- **Local Access Required:** Local agents (international travel to Russia is heavily restricted/advised against).
- **Risks & Constraints:** Severe sanctions compliance risks, payment channel blocks, reputational risks for DealyVIP, legal risks depending on DealyVIP's jurisdiction of operation.

### Service Analysis (Russia)
- **Service:** Business Matchmaking & Trade Coordination
  - **Delivery Model:** NOT AVAILABLE (Pending Legal Review)
  - **Required Capability:** Compliance with all applicable sanctions regimes.
  - **Evidence:** N/A
  - **Risk:** Extreme legal and sanctions risk.
  - **Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED (to determine if any activity is legally permissible)
  - **Next Validation Step:** Obtain formal legal counsel regarding sanctions compliance before offering any trade-related services.

- **Service:** Company Verification
  - **Delivery Model:** Direct (Remote)
  - **Required Capability:** Access to Spark-Interfax or EGRUL (Federal Tax Service).
  - **Evidence:** NO VERIFIED EVIDENCE
  - **Risk:** Medium (Access to registries may be blocked from outside).
  - **Status:** RESEARCH REQUIRED
  - **Next Validation Step:** Determine legal and technical ability to query Russian registries.
