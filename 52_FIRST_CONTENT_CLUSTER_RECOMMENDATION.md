# First Content Cluster Recommendation (Sprint 6.1)

Based on the research, only 4 of the original 6 candidates are recommended to proceed to production to avoid overlap and maintain high quality.

## 1. How to Verify a Turkish Company
- **Target Audience:** Foreign buyers, importers, and investors.
- **Primary Problem:** Cannot access e-Devlet to verify MERSİS records remotely.
- **Why it matters:** Foundational trust piece. Establishes DealyVIP's expertise in Turkish bureaucracy.
- **Research Readiness:** High.
- **Risk Level:** Low (If kept to procedural explanations).

## 2. Supplier & Factory Verification in Türkiye
- **Target Audience:** Physical goods importers.
- **Primary Problem:** Distinguishing between actual manufacturers and trading companies to prevent fraud.
- **Why it matters:** Leads directly into the Local Assistance funnel (coordinating factory visits).
- **Research Readiness:** High.
- **Risk Level:** Medium (Must clearly distinguish between observation visits and licensed QC inspections).

## 3. How to Verify a Ukrainian Company
- **Target Audience:** Foreign buyers and aid/reconstruction organizations.
- **Primary Problem:** Finding reliable data in a restricted, wartime digital environment.
- **Why it matters:** Establishes the EDRPOU concept and builds trust through transparency regarding martial law limitations.
- **Research Readiness:** High.
- **Risk Level:** Medium (Requires time-sensitive disclaimers regarding registry uptime).

## 4. Coordinating Business Assistance Between Türkiye and Ukraine
- **Target Audience:** Cross-border business operators needing physical logistics and proxy representation.
- **Primary Problem:** Overcoming travel bans, language barriers, and lack of physical presence.
- **Why it matters:** Directly drives the core value proposition of DealyVIP (Coordination).
- **Research Readiness:** High.
- **Risk Level:** Low (Provided no guarantees of service availability are made).

*Note: The original candidates "How to Start Doing Business in Ukraine" and "Supplier Verification Before Buying from Türkiye" were merged or discarded due to overlap or excessive scope.*
