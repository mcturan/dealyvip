# Operational Capability Model

## Purpose
Define the standard model for how DealyVIP assesses, categorizes, and delivers its services. This prevents unsupported claims by establishing a strict evidence requirement before any service is presented to users.

## Service Delivery Types
1. **Direct Delivery:** DealyVIP provides the service directly (e.g., providing curated information).
2. **Coordination:** DealyVIP organizes the service, but a third-party partner or specialist executes the regulated or localized component.
3. **Referral:** DealyVIP connects the customer to a verified professional but does not manage the delivery.

## Capability Statuses
- **ACTIVE:** DealyVIP can currently deliver the service with a realistic, known operational path and verified evidence.
- **COORDINATED:** DealyVIP can reliably coordinate the service, but another qualified party performs the regulated or specialist component. Evidence of the partner relationship is required.
- **PILOT:** There is a credible delivery path, but it has not yet been sufficiently tested for routine use.
- **RESEARCH REQUIRED:** The idea is plausible but evidence or operational details are insufficient.
- **NOT AVAILABLE:** The service cannot or should not currently be offered (e.g., due to regulatory constraints, lack of partners, or strategic exclusion).

## Evidence Standard
Every ACTIVE or COORDINATED capability must have concrete evidence recorded in the Evidence Register. 
Acceptable evidence includes:
- Known, verified local contact
- Established partner or vendor relationship
- Verified professional network (lawyers, accountants, etc.)
- Documented operational procedure that has been successfully tested
- Demonstrated ability to execute (e.g., prior successful delivery)

A generic internet search result or the mere existence of a service provider in a target country does **not** constitute evidence.
