# Practical Assistance Content Standard

This standard applies to all guides in the "Local Assistance & Travel" and "Supplier Search & Matchmaking" categories.

1. **Coordination vs. Execution:** The content must clearly distinguish between logistical coordination (which DealyVIP supports) and certified execution (which requires licensed lawyers or ISO-accredited QA inspectors).
2. **Proxy vs. Certification:** An observation visit is strictly defined as an independent professional acting as a buyer's "eyes and ears." It is never to be referred to as an "official inspection" or "quality certification."
3. **Language Neutrality:** The content must address both sides of a transaction neutrally (e.g., preparing for an interpreter), avoiding bias toward either the buyer or the seller.
