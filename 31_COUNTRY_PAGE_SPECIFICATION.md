# Country Page Specification

## Purpose
Define the standard structure of a country page, acting as an informational hub for that specific market.

## Standard Structure

1. **Hero / Country Introduction:**
   - E.g., "Doing Business in Türkiye"
   - Brief 2-3 paragraph orientation of the market climate.
   
2. **Country-Specific Limitations / Risks (Crucial):**
   - Highlighting major structural issues immediately (e.g., Sanctions for Russia, Martial Law for Ukraine, currency volatility for Türkiye).

3. **Common Business Questions:**
   - Accordion or short-answer list of the most frequent questions, linking out to full guides.

4. **Verification and Trust Hub:**
   - Summary of how verification works in this country, linking to specific country verification guides.

5. **Supplier and Manufacturer Discovery Hub:**
   - Summary of sourcing landscape, linking to sourcing guides.

6. **Business Visits & Communication Hub:**
   - Information on travel, visas, and language.

7. **Relevant Practical Guides:**
   - Grid of guides heavily tagged for this country.

8. **Related Assistance:**
   - Teaser for local coordination services available in this country.

9. **Contact CTA:**
   - "Need assistance in [Country]? Contact us."

## Rule
Do not duplicate the entire text of a guide on the country page. The country page must serve as a router to the detailed guide pages.
