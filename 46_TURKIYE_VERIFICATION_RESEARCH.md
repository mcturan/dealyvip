# Türkiye Verification Research

## 1. Company Verification (Legal & Administrative)
- **MERSİS (Central Registration System):**
  - **URL:** `mersis.ticaret.gov.tr`
  - **Purpose:** Official system managed by the Ministry of Trade.
  - **Accessibility:** Basic searches (existence, status) can be public, but full access requires e-Devlet (e-Government) login, which foreign nationals typically cannot access without a Turkish ID or residence permit.
- **Trade Registry Gazette (Ticaret Sicil Gazetesi):**
  - **URL:** `ticaretsicil.gov.tr`
  - **Purpose:** Verifying official corporate announcements, structure changes, and board appointments.
- **Tax Identification Number (VKN):**
  - Can be verified via the Revenue Administration (`ivd.gib.gov.tr`) if the correct tax office is known.
- **Chambers of Commerce:**
  - E.g., Istanbul Chamber of Commerce (ITO). Useful for verifying active registration.

## 2. Supplier & Product Verification (Commercial)
- Distinguishing between a true manufacturer and a trading company (intermediary) is a primary user problem.
- **Capacity Reports (Kapasite Raporu):** Official documents issued by chambers of commerce proving a factory's actual manufacturing capability. Buyers should request this document from suppliers.
- **Customs & Export Records:** Not publicly searchable by third parties due to commercial confidentiality.

## 3. Factory Visit and Inspection
- **Definition:** Physical on-site checks.
- **DealyVIP Relevance:** DealyVIP can coordinate independent professionals to visit locations in Türkiye.
- **Legal Boundaries:** Factory visits for general observation/coordination do not require a license. However, issuing official ISO-certified Quality Control (QC) or formal SGS-style technical product inspections requires licensed inspection agencies. DealyVIP coordinates visits, it does not issue accredited QC certificates.

## Limitations to State
- A company being legally registered does not guarantee it is a reliable or debt-free supplier.
- Foreigners face digital barriers (lack of e-Devlet) when trying to perform deep due diligence on Turkish companies remotely.
