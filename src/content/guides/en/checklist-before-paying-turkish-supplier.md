Sending an international wire transfer is often the point of no return in cross-border trade. Before you authorize a payment to a Turkish supplier, you should complete a thorough review of the transaction details.

*Note: This is a general due diligence checklist. It is not legal or financial advice. Completing this checklist does not guarantee fraud prevention, but it significantly reduces your risk profile.*

## 1. Company Identity & Registration
- [ ] Have you verified the company's existence in **MERSİS** or the **Trade Registry Gazette**?
- [ ] Do you have a copy of their recent **Vergi Levhası** (Tax Plate) and **Faaliyet Belgesi** (Activity Certificate)?
- [ ] Does the company name on the proforma invoice match the official registry records exactly?

## 2. Representative Authority
- [ ] Have you identified who is signing the contract or invoice?
- [ ] Have you received the company's **İmza Sirküleri** (Signature Circular)?
- [ ] Is the person signing legally authorized to bind the company according to the circular?

## 3. Bank Account Consistency
- [ ] Is the bank account located in Türkiye?
- [ ] Does the beneficiary name on the bank account match the **exact legal corporate name**? *(Never send money to an individual employee, a shareholder's personal account, or a third-party logistics firm).*
- [ ] Have the payment instructions been confirmed via a live phone call or video call? *(Do not rely solely on email, as Business Email Compromise is a common global threat).*

## 4. Product and Factory Verification
- [ ] Have you requested and tested a physical sample of the product?
- [ ] If they claim to be a manufacturer, have you seen their **Kapasite Raporu** (Capacity Report)?
- [ ] Have you conducted a live video tour of the production facility, or hired a local agent to perform a physical inspection?

## 5. Commercial Terms and Documentation
- [ ] Is the incoterm (e.g., FOB, EXW, CIF) clearly stated and mutually understood?
- [ ] Is the payment structure balanced? (e.g., using a Letter of Credit, or a milestone-based T/T deposit rather than 100% upfront).
- [ ] Does the proforma invoice clearly outline the product specifications, delivery timeline, and penalties for delay?

## 6. Independent Checks and Escalation
- [ ] Have you performed a basic web search for the company name alongside keywords like "şikayet" (complaint) or "dolandırıcılık" (fraud)?
- [ ] If this is a high-value transaction, have you reached your **escalation threshold**? (i.e., Is it time to hire a local Turkish commercial lawyer to review the contract or a third-party QC agency to inspect the goods before the final balance is paid?)

**General Due Diligence Practice:** If any item on this checklist raises a red flag, pause the transaction. Do not allow a supplier to pressure you into a rushed payment due to "expiring discounts" or "urgent material costs."
