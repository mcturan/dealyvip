Before engaging in cross-border business, it is essential to verify the legal existence of your potential Turkish partner. **Company verification in Türkiye means confirming that the entity is officially registered with the government and legally permitted to conduct business.** 

This guide explains how international buyers can use official Turkish registries to verify basic corporate information.

## What You Can Verify Publicly

Using official Turkish state and statutory portals, you can generally confirm:
- The legal name of the company.
- The company's Tax Identification Number (Vergi Kimlik Numarası - VKN) or MERSİS number.
- The registered headquarters address.
- The current operational status (e.g., active or liquidated).

## What You May Not Be Able to Verify Publicly

Without a Turkish citizen identity number (TC Kimlik) and access to the authenticated e-Devlet (e-Government) portal, international users **cannot** easily access:
- The complete historical list of board members and shareholders.
- Detailed financial statements or tax debt status.
- Internal corporate restructuring documents.

## Step-by-Step Verification Workflow

### 1. Request Official Documentation
Always ask the supplier for their foundational documents before you attempt a public search. Specifically, ask for:
- **Vergi Levhası** (Tax Registration Certificate): Contains their Tax ID (VKN) and Tax Office.
- **Ticaret Sicil Gazetesi** (Trade Registry Gazette publication): Shows their official establishment.
- **Faaliyet Belgesi** (Certificate of Activity): Issued by the local Chamber of Commerce, proving they are currently active.

### 2. Search MERSİS (Central Registration System)
MERSİS is the Ministry of Trade's central database. You can perform a basic search on `mersis.ticaret.gov.tr` using the company name or MERSİS number to confirm the company exists and matches the documents you were given.

### 3. Check the Trade Registry Gazette
Go to `ticaretsicil.gov.tr`. While viewing detailed PDFs requires a free account registration, you can search the index to ensure the company has a legitimate trail of official announcements (like address changes or board appointments).

### 4. Verify Tax Registration
If you have the company's VKN and their registered Tax Office (Vergi Dairesi), you can use the Revenue Administration's (GİB) Interactive Tax Office portal (`ivd.gib.gov.tr`) to query the validity of their tax plate (e-Vergi Levhası). 

## Warning Signs

During your verification, watch out for these red flags:
- The company name on the proforma invoice does not perfectly match the name in MERSİS.
- The supplier refuses to provide a Vergi Levhası or claims they "do not need one for exports."
- The registered address points to a virtual office or residential apartment when they claim to own a massive factory.
- The MERSİS status is listed as "Terkin" (Liquidated) or "Tasfiye Halinde" (In Liquidation).

## What Verification Cannot Guarantee

Finding a company in an official registry **does not guarantee** that the company is a safe business partner. Registration proves legal existence; it does not prove:
- **Creditworthiness or financial health.**
- **Manufacturing capability.** A registered "trade company" might outsource 100% of its production.
- **Product quality or export compliance.**

## When Local Assistance May Be Useful

If you are dealing with a high-value order or face language barriers navigating Turkish official and registry sites, you may need a local professional (such as a Turkish lawyer, accountant, or commercial investigator) to pull full authenticated records via e-Devlet and perform physical site visits.
