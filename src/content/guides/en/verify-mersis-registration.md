When conducting business with a Turkish entity, you will frequently encounter a **MERSİS number**. MERSİS (Merkezi Sicil Kayıt Sistemi) is the Central Registration System operated by the Turkish Ministry of Trade. 

This guide explains how to use a MERSİS number to verify a company's identity and outlines the limits of public access for international users.

## What is a MERSİS Number?

A MERSİS number is a unique 16-digit identification code assigned to commercial enterprises, limited companies, joint-stock companies, and foreign company branches registered in Türkiye. 

For limited (Ltd. Şti.) and joint-stock (A.Ş.) companies, the MERSİS number almost always begins with a `0`, followed by the company's 10-digit Tax Identification Number (VKN), and ends with `00015` or a similar sequence (e.g., `0123456789000015`).

## How to Check a MERSİS Number

The official portal for MERSİS is `mersis.ticaret.gov.tr`. 

1. Navigate to the MERSİS portal.
2. Look for the public inquiry section (often labeled "Firma Sorgulama" or Company Search).
3. Enter the 16-digit MERSİS number or the exact company name.

### What Public Lookup Reveals
A basic, unauthenticated public search will typically confirm:
- That the MERSİS number is valid.
- The official legal name of the entity.
- The city of registration.
- The basic operational status (Active/Inactive).

## Limitations on Public Access

It is important to understand that **not all company information is freely available to the global public.**

While anyone can verify that a MERSİS number belongs to a specific company, **full access to detailed corporate records requires authentication.** To view the detailed list of authorized signatories, complete historical address changes, and capital structures, a user must log in.

Logging into MERSİS requires an **e-Devlet (e-Government) password**, which is tied to a Turkish National Identity Number (TC Kimlik Numarası) or a registered foreigner ID. 

### What This Means for International Buyers

If you are an international buyer without Turkish residency:
- You **can** perform a basic surface-level verification to ensure the company exists and the name matches your invoices.
- You **cannot** independently extract detailed structural or historical documents directly from MERSİS.

## Practical Alternatives

If you require deeper verification but do not have e-Devlet access:

1. **Ask the Supplier:** Request that the supplier download and send you their recent Trade Registry Gazette (Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi) publications and their Chamber of Commerce Activity Certificate (Faaliyet Belgesi). You can then cross-reference the numbers on these documents with the public MERSİS lookup.
2. **Use Local Assistance:** If the transaction carries high risk, engage a local Turkish attorney or a verified local commercial consultant who possesses the necessary e-Devlet access to conduct a comprehensive background check on your behalf.
