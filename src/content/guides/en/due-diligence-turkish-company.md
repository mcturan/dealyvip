Finding a company in an official database is only the first step. **Due diligence** is the comprehensive process of ensuring the entity you are talking to is capable, legitimate, and safe to transact with.

This checklist provides a structured framework for international buyers evaluating a Turkish business partner.

## Due Diligence Checklist

### 1. Identity & Registration Alignment
- [ ] Have you obtained the company's **Vergi Levhası** (Tax Plate)?
- [ ] Have you obtained a recent **Faaliyet Belgesi** (Chamber of Commerce Activity Certificate)?
- [ ] Does the exact legal name on these documents match the name in the **MERSİS** database?
- [ ] Does the name match the beneficiary on the proforma invoice?

### 2. Physical Address & Operations
- [ ] Have you verified the registered headquarters address using Google Maps/Street View?
- [ ] Does the legal address align with the nature of the business? (e.g., A factory should be in an industrial zone, not a residential apartment).
- [ ] Have you conducted a live video call of the production facility?
- [ ] Have you requested a **Kapasite Raporu** (Capacity Report) if they claim to be the direct manufacturer?

### 3. Financial & Payment Consistency
- [ ] Is the bank account provided for payment physically located in Türkiye? 
- [ ] Is the bank account strictly in the name of the registered corporate entity? *(Never pay an individual or an unrelated offshore third-party company).*
- [ ] Have you agreed on a balanced payment structure (e.g., Letter of Credit, or a split deposit/balance transfer)?

### 4. Digital Footprint
- [ ] Does the company have a professional web presence?
- [ ] Do the email addresses used for communication match the official domain? *(Be highly suspicious of generic Gmail or Yahoo accounts for corporate trade).*
- [ ] Are the contact numbers legitimate Turkish lines (+90)? 

## What Online Verification Can and Cannot Do

**Online verification can:**
- Confirm a company was legally formed.
- Verify its official tax identification number.
- Confirm its registered headquarters.

**Online verification CANNOT:**
- Prove that the person emailing you actually works for that company.
- Prove that the company is currently solvent and not facing bankruptcy.
- Guarantee that the goods shipped will match the quality of the sample.

## When to Seek Professional Local Assistance

If you are negotiating a high-value contract, purchasing specialized machinery, or if your basic checks raise red flags, online due diligence is no longer sufficient.

In these scenarios, it is highly recommended to engage:
- **A Turkish Commercial Lawyer:** To draft binding bilingual contracts and perform deep, authenticated background checks (including pending litigation or tax debts).
- **A Third-Party Inspection Agency:** To visit the factory, verify raw materials, and conduct pre-shipment quality inspections (e.g., SGS, Intertek, or local equivalents).
