Navigating the Turkish manufacturing and export market entirely from overseas can be challenging due to language barriers, localized business culture, and the limitations of online verification. 

When digital due diligence is not enough, engaging local professionals becomes a necessity. This guide outlines the main categories of practical business assistance you may need when doing business in Türkiye.

## Categories of Local Assistance

### 1. Translation and Communication Support
Many Turkish manufacturers, especially small-to-medium enterprises (SMEs) located outside of major export hubs, may not have fluent English-speaking sales teams.
- **What it provides:** Accurate translation of technical specifications, real-time interpretation during video calls, and cultural mediation to ensure both sides understand the contract terms.

### 2. Factory and Supplier Visits
If you are placing a significant order, physical verification is the only way to confirm a supplier’s true manufacturing capacity.
- **What it provides:** A local representative travels to the factory in industrial zones (like Bursa, Gaziantep, or Denizli) to take live video, verify the machinery, and confirm the business is operating as claimed.

### 3. Legal and Regulatory Due Diligence
Online databases like MERSİS are restricted. Full records require e-Devlet authentication.
- **What it provides:** A registered Turkish attorney can access detailed corporate histories, check for pending bankruptcies, identify active litigation, and review the legal standing of the company's board members.

### 4. Quality Control and Pre-Shipment Inspection
Discovering that a product does not meet your specifications after it arrives in your home country is a costly mistake.
- **What it provides:** Local quality control (QC) agents or third-party inspection firms can physically inspect the goods at the factory or port before the final payment is released and the container is sealed.
