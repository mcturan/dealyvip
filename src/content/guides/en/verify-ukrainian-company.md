Verifying a business partner in Ukraine requires understanding the state registry system and navigating the access restrictions put in place during martial law.

## What This Guide Helps You Check
- The legal existence of a Ukrainian entity.
- The company's registered address and authorized director.
- How to use the EDRPOU code for due diligence.

## What Public Information Cannot Establish
- **Wartime Operational Capacity:** A company may be legally active but physically displaced or non-operational due to the conflict.
- **Logistical Feasibility:** The registry does not indicate whether the company can successfully export goods under current border conditions.

## The EDRPOU Code
The cornerstone of Ukrainian business verification is the **EDRPOU code** (ЄДРПОУ). This is a unique 8-digit identification number assigned to every legal entity. 
*Action:* Always ask your Ukrainian counterparty for their EDRPOU code. Company names can be translated inconsistently into English, but the 8-digit code never changes.

## Step-by-Step Verification Process

### 1. Request Company Details
Ask for the company's full legal name in Ukrainian and their 8-digit EDRPOU code.

### 2. Check the Unified State Register (USR)
The official government database is the **Unified State Register** managed by the Ministry of Justice. Searching the EDRPOU code here will confirm if the company is registered, active, or in the process of liquidation.

### 3. Use Aggregator Platforms
Because the official government registry UI can be difficult for foreigners to navigate, many businesses rely on reputable third-party intelligence platforms like **Opendatabot** or **YouControl**. These platforms aggregate state registry data, court records, and tax debt information into easily readable dossiers.

## Red Flags
- The company refuses to provide an EDRPOU code.
- The authorized director listed in the registry does not match the person signing your contract.
- The company has a status of "in the process of termination" in the registry.

## Important Limitations (Time-Sensitive)
**Martial Law Restrictions:** Access to the official Unified State Register fluctuates. The Ukrainian government occasionally restricts public access to protect sensitive data during the war. You may be required to solve CAPTCHAs, use a VPN if geo-blocked, or possess a Ukrainian Electronic Digital Signature (KEP/EDS) for deep searches.
