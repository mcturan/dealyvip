# Website Information Architecture

## Purpose
Define the complete logical website tree, demonstrating the relationships between page groups.

## Architecture Tree

**1. CORE PAGES**
- Home (`/`)
- About DealyVIP (`/about`)
- Contact (`/contact`)

**2. COUNTRY PAGES (Hubs)**
- Türkiye Business Guide (`/turkiye`)
- Ukraine Business Guide (`/ukraine`)
- Russia Compliance Overview (`/russia`)
- Iran Compliance Overview (`/iran`)

**3. GUIDE / QUESTION PAGES**
- **Verification & Trust** (`/guides/verification/`)
  - How to Verify a Company in Türkiye
  - How to Verify a Company in Ukraine
  - Verifying Factory Physical Existence
  - Limits of Corporate Registry Verification
  - Verifying Russian Entities (Compliance Context)
- **Sourcing & Suppliers** (`/guides/sourcing/`)
  - Finding Reliable Suppliers in Türkiye
  - Sourcing from Ukraine Safely
  - Sourcing from Iran (Exemptions & Constraints)
  - Essential Documents from New Suppliers
- **Market Entry & Logistics** (`/guides/operations/`)
  - Turkish Company Setup Basics
  - Turkish Business Visa Basics
  - Turkish Export Logistics & Customs Basics
  - Russia Business Compliance & Exemptions
- **Cross-Border Basics** (`/guides/basics/`)
  - Overseas Supplier Pre-Payment Checklist
  - Cross-Border Banking Orientation
  - Cross-Border Communication Mistakes
  - Preparing for an Overseas Factory Visit

**4. LOCAL ASSISTANCE PAGES**
- **Türkiye** (`/assistance/turkiye/`)
  - Local Business Assistance in Türkiye
  - Turkish Business Interpreter Coordination
- **Ukraine** (`/assistance/ukraine/`)
  - Local Business Assistance in Ukraine
- **General** (`/assistance/`)
  - Arranging Local Business Meetings
  - Finding Qualified Business Interpreters

**5. LEGAL / TRUST PAGES**
- Privacy Policy (`/privacy`)
- Terms of Use (`/terms`)
- Disclaimer / Scope of Service (`/disclaimer`)

## Language Structure
The base architecture is English (`/en/` or root). Localized pages will map to identical structures under their language prefix (e.g., `/tr/guides/...`, `/ru/guides/...`) only where justified by the Language Strategy (avoiding mass-translation).
