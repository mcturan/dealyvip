# Contact Page Specification

## Purpose
Define a simple, lead-qualifying contact method without complex ticketing or CRM workflows.

## User Input Encouragement
Users should be prompted (via form fields or email template instructions) to provide:
- **Country of Interest:** (Türkiye, Ukraine, Russia, Iran)
- **Primary Objective:** What they are trying to do.
- **Company/Industry:** Context for the request.
- **Location:** Specific city/region involved.
- **Timeline:** Urgency of the request.
- **Specific Question/Requirement:** Open text block.
- **Preferred Communication Language.**
- **Contact Information:** Name, Email, Phone/WhatsApp.

## Privacy & Minimum-Data Principles
- Request only what is necessary to evaluate the feasibility of assistance.
- No mandatory account creation to contact DealyVIP.
- Clear link to Privacy Policy next to the submit action.
- Data should be securely routed to an internal business inbox.
