# Operational Risks

## Purpose
Identify and categorize the risks associated with DealyVIP's operational capabilities across the target markets.

## 1. Legal and Regulatory Risks
- **Sanctions Compliance (Russia & Iran):** Both Russia and Iran are subject to comprehensive U.S. (OFAC) and international sanctions. Engaging in business matching or trade coordination may violate sanctions, even if indirect.
  - *Source:* U.S. Department of the Treasury (OFAC)
  - *Date Checked:* 2026-08-21
- **Unauthorized Practice of Law/Finance:** Providing legal or financial advice without local licenses exposes DealyVIP to severe legal penalties. All such services must be strictly referred to licensed partners.

## 2. Safety and Logistics Risks
- **Physical Safety (Ukraine):** Ukraine operates under martial law due to ongoing conflict. Physical verification visits (e.g., to factories) carry extreme physical risks for field agents and disrupted local logistics.
  - *Source:* International Travel Advisories
  - *Date Checked:* 2026-08-21
- **Travel Restrictions (Russia/Iran):** Travel to these jurisdictions is heavily restricted or advised against by Western governments, severely limiting DealyVIP's ability to deploy internal staff for oversight.

## 3. Financial and Fraud Risks
- **Payment Channel Blocks:** Standard international banking channels (SWIFT) are largely disconnected from Russia and Iran (FATF blacklisted/sanctioned). DealyVIP cannot facilitate payments or coordinate financial transactions in these markets without violating international banking laws.
- **Supplier Fraud:** B2B matchmaking carries the inherent risk that a supplier may defraud a foreign buyer. DealyVIP faces reputational risk if a coordinated supplier fails to deliver.

## 4. Third-Party Dependency Risks
- **Partner Reliability:** DealyVIP's "Coordination" model relies entirely on third-party field agents, lawyers, and logistics providers. Poor performance by a partner directly damages DealyVIP's "Trust" value proposition.
- **Language Barriers:** Misinterpretation of official documents or business meetings by unqualified interpreters can lead to critical business failures for clients.

## 5. Reputational Risks
- **False Guarantees:** Any perception that DealyVIP "guarantees" a supplier or a commercial outcome creates massive liability if the deal fails. The boundaries of verification must be strictly defined (e.g., "We verify the factory exists," not "We guarantee they will ship quality goods").
