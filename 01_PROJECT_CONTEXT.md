# Project Context

## Purpose
Define the durable context of the DealyVIP project so that humans and AI agents can understand what the project is, why it exists, and what stage it is in.

## Confirmed Context
DealyVIP is being developed as an international business gateway focused initially on Türkiye, Ukraine, Russia, and Iran.

The project is not intended to become a payment platform, marketplace, or complex self-service portal in its initial form.

The primary customer journey is:

Information → Trust → Contact

The intended role is to help people and companies navigate cross-border business by providing information, local access, verification, coordination, and appropriate professional connections where genuinely available.

## Working Principle
Only capabilities that can actually be delivered, directly or through clearly identified and reliable arrangements, may be represented as available.
