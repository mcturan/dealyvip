# Customer Problems

## Purpose
Define the real problems DealyVIP should solve before services or website pages are designed.

## Prioritized Customer Problems

### 1. Verification and Trust
- "Is this company real?"
- "Does this factory actually exist?"
- "Can someone physically visit the location for me to verify?"

### 2. Partner Discovery
- "How can I find a reliable supplier or business partner?"

### 3. Market Entry Guidance
- "I want to do business in this country but do not know where to start."

### 4. Coordination and Logistics
- "I need help coordinating local professionals and business activities."
- "I need practical support while visiting the country."

## Sprint Requirement
Refined into prioritized customer problems without inventing customer research results. All listed problems align with the Information → Trust → Contact journey.
