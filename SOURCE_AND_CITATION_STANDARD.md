# Source and Citation Standard

## Source Priority Model
1. **Official Government or Regulatory Source** (e.g., Ministries, Customs)
2. **Official Registry or Institutional Source** (e.g., Trade Chambers)
3. **Recognized International Organization** (e.g., World Bank, OECD)
4. **Primary Authoritative Source** (e.g., Major legal firms, established banks)
5. **Carefully Selected Secondary Source** (e.g., Verified industry publications)

## Implementation Rules
- **No Invented Citations:** Never invent a URL or a source name.
- **Traceability:** Factual claims about legal requirements, registry structures, or official processes must be directly attributable.
- **URL Stability:** Use stable, high-level URLs rather than deeply nested session-dependent links if they are prone to expiry.
- **Limitations Acknowledgment:** If a source is officially recognized but practically inaccessible (e.g., requires local e-signature), this must be explicitly documented.
