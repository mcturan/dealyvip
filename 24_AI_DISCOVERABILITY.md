# AI Discoverability Strategy

## Purpose
Ensure DealyVIP content is highly useful to AI search systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, etc.) and traditional search engines without relying on keyword stuffing or "AI SEO tricks."

## Practical Content Rules

1. **Direct Answers First:** Every page must begin with a clear, concise answer to the primary question. AI models favor content that doesn't bury the lede.
2. **Semantic Headings:** Use logical `H1`, `H2`, and `H3` structures. Questions should often be headers, followed immediately by the answer.
3. **Entity Clarity:** Use precise names for countries, registries, documents, and processes. Avoid vague pronouns. 
4. **Country Specificity:** Do not mix advice for Türkiye and Ukraine in the same paragraph unless explicitly comparing them. 
5. **Visible Dates:** Include a "Last Updated: [Date]" tag on all pages, especially for sanctions, visas, or regulations, as AI systems prioritize fresh, time-stamped facts.
6. **Source Handling:** When citing regulations (e.g., OFAC), mention the source explicitly in the text.
7. **Author/Editorial Responsibility:** Maintain a consistent, professional, objective tone that establishes authority.
8. **Structured Data:** Recommend using standard JSON-LD Schema (e.g., `FAQPage`, `Article`) when the website is eventually built.
