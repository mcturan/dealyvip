# Country Content Model

## Purpose
Define the specific content strategy for each of the four target markets. Avoids artificial symmetry by focusing only on what matters.

## 1. Türkiye
- **Major User Questions:** How to verify a company? How to find a supplier? How to set up a company? Need an interpreter?
- **Relevant Audiences:** Foreign buyers, investors, visiting executives.
- **Useful Languages:** English, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian.
- **Recommended Priorities:** Supplier search, company verification, local assistance.
- **Country-Specific Risks:** Currency volatility, changing foreign investment rules.
- **Content Limitations:** Cannot offer legal advice on setup, only orientation.

## 2. Ukraine
- **Major User Questions:** How to verify a company? Is it safe to travel? How to find manufacturers safely?
- **Relevant Audiences:** Investors, buyers navigating martial law constraints.
- **Useful Languages:** English, Ukrainian.
- **Recommended Priorities:** Verification under constraint, safety/risk overview.
- **Country-Specific Risks:** Extreme physical risk, martial law, logistics disruption.
- **Content Limitations:** Must explicitly warn against uncoordinated physical visits.

## 3. Russia
- **Major User Questions:** What are the sanctions? Can I verify a Russian entity?
- **Relevant Audiences:** International compliance officers, legacy business owners.
- **Useful Languages:** English, Russian.
- **Recommended Priorities:** Sanctions overview, remote entity verification limits.
- **Country-Specific Risks:** Severe OFAC/EU sanctions, banking blocks.
- **Content Limitations:** Cannot offer any trade facilitation, matchmaking, or local assistance. Focus strictly on objective compliance/risk information.

## 4. Iran
- **Major User Questions:** What are the sanctions? Can I legally source from Iran?
- **Relevant Audiences:** International compliance officers.
- **Useful Languages:** English, Persian.
- **Recommended Priorities:** Sanctions overview.
- **Country-Specific Risks:** Severe OFAC/FATF sanctions.
- **Content Limitations:** Same as Russia. Strictly informational regarding restrictions.
