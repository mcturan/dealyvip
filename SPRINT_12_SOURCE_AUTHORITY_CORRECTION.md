# Sprint 12 Source Authority Correction

This document re-evaluates and strictly classifies the sources used in Sprint 11 and earlier, adhering to the rigorous Sprint 12 evidence standards.

## MERSİS (Central Registration System)
- **Previous Classification:** "Official" / "Government"
- **Corrected Classification:** `OFFICIAL_PRIMARY`
- **Evidence for Authority:** MERSİS is the definitive central registry mandated by the Turkish Commercial Code (TTK) and operated directly by the Ministry of Trade (Ticaret Bakanlığı).
- **Verification Method:** Source documentation on `mersis.ticaret.gov.tr`.
- **Remaining Uncertainty:** None regarding authority. Limitations relate only to public access vs. authenticated (e-Devlet) access.
- **Pages Affected:** verify-turkish-company.md, verify-mersis-registration.md, due-diligence-turkish-company.md

## Turkish Trade Registry Gazette (Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi)
- **Previous Classification:** "Official" / "Government"
- **Corrected Classification:** `STATUTORY_OR_REGULATED_INSTITUTION`
- **Evidence for Authority:** The Gazette is operated by TOBB (Union of Chambers and Commodity Exchanges of Turkey), a statutory body established by law (Law No. 5174). It acts as the legal public record for trade registry announcements, but TOBB is a public professional organization, not a direct government ministry.
- **Verification Method:** Legislative mandate check (Turkish Commercial Code Article 35).
- **Remaining Uncertainty:** None.
- **Pages Affected:** verify-turkish-company.md, due-diligence-turkish-company.md (Mentions of it being a "government portal" must be corrected to "official statutory registry").

## GİB e-Archive Invoice Verification (e-Arşiv Fatura Sorgulama)
- **Previous Classification:** "Official" / "Government"
- **Corrected Classification:** `OFFICIAL_PRIMARY`
- **Evidence for Authority:** Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı (GİB) is the Revenue Administration under the Ministry of Treasury and Finance.
- **Verification Method:** Source documentation on `gib.gov.tr` and `efatura.gov.tr`.
- **Remaining Uncertainty:** None.
- **Pages Affected:** check-turkish-e-invoice.md

## GİB Tax Plate Verification (e-Vergi Levhası Sorgulama)
- **Previous Classification:** "Official" / "Government"
- **Corrected Classification:** `OFFICIAL_PRIMARY`
- **Evidence for Authority:** Operated directly by the Revenue Administration.
- **Verification Method:** Source documentation on `ivd.gib.gov.tr`.
- **Remaining Uncertainty:** None.
- **Pages Affected:** due-diligence-turkish-company.md, verify-turkish-company.md
