# Roadmap

## Purpose
Provide the ordered development roadmap.

## Sprint 1 — Project Identity and Working System
Current sprint.
Outputs:
- project context,
- principles,
- vision and positioning,
- working method,
- target markets,
- capability matrix,
- customer problems,
- value proposition,
- scope and boundaries,
- customer archetypes.

## Sprint 2 — Market Capability and Service Discovery
Validate realistic capabilities, delivery paths, partners, and service availability.

## Sprint 3 — Service Catalog
Define approved services, deliverables, exclusions, workflows, country availability, and risk notes.

## Sprint 4 — Brand Strategy
Define naming evaluation, positioning language, tone, trust architecture, and visual direction.

## Sprint 5 — Information Architecture
Define sitemap, navigation, country/service/content model, contact paths, URL logic.

## Sprint 6 — Language and Content Strategy
Define target-language matrix, localization rules, templates, and content priorities.

## Sprint 7 — Research, SEO, and AI Discoverability Standards
Define source hierarchy, fact review, update policy, structured data approach, answer-oriented content structure, and discoverability practices.

## Sprint 8 — Content Inventory and Production Plan
Prioritize high-intent pages and create a controlled content backlog.

## Sprint 9 — Technical Architecture
Select the simplest maintainable static-first implementation after content requirements are known.

## Sprint 10 — Design and Prototype
Create mobile-first page patterns and high-trust homepage direction.

## Sprint 11 — Implementation
Build the approved website.

## Sprint 12 — QA and Launch Preparation
Perform functional, responsive, accessibility, performance, content, and deployment checks.
