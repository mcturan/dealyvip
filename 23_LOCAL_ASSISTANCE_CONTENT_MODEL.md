# Local Assistance Content Model

## Purpose
Defines how DealyVIP's local assistance services are described on the website, ensuring honest and bounded claims.

## Appropriate Wording
- "We explain how this process works."
- "If you need local assistance, contact us to discuss your requirements."
- "We coordinate with local specialists to facilitate your visit."

## Availability Limitations
Every local assistance page MUST include a statement similar to:
*Availability of physical coordination depends on exact location, current local conditions, and specific requirements.*

## What Users Should Include When Contacting Us
Pages should explicitly ask users to provide:
- Their exact objective.
- Specific city/region.
- Desired timeline.
- Specific language requirements.

## Example CTAs
- **Good:** "Contact us to discuss your local assistance needs."
- **Good:** "Request a consultation for interpreter availability."
- **Bad:** "Book an interpreter now." (Implies e-commerce/guaranteed immediate availability).
- **Bad:** "We guarantee your factory visit will be successful."
