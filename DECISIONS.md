## Initial Decisions
### DEC-001 — Sprint-based project execution
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Status: Accepted
- Decision: The project will be developed through reviewable sprints rather than ad hoc tasks.
- Rationale: Reduce scope drift and improve traceability.
- Consequences: Each sprint requires explicit scope and an audit.

### DEC-002 — No payment platform in the initial website
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Status: Accepted
- Decision: The initial website will guide users toward contact rather than online purchasing.
- Rationale: The primary business objective is trust-building and qualified contact generation.

### DEC-003 — Initial markets
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Status: Accepted
- Decision: Initial focus is Türkiye, Ukraine, Russia, and Iran.
- Rationale: These are the markets currently selected for the project.

### DEC-004 — Service Availability Validation
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Status: VALIDATION REQUIRED
- Decision: Genuinely available services in Türkiye, Ukraine, Russia, and Iran must be operationally validated before being listed as active.
- Rationale: Project principle of "No unsupported claims" and "Country inclusion does not equal service availability".
- Consequences: Website service claims are blocked until the Country Capability Matrix is updated with validated delivery paths.
