# Beta Website Scope

## Purpose
Explicitly define what the DealyVIP beta website will and will not contain to prevent scope creep and overengineering.

## What the Beta WILL Contain
- A static-first informational architecture.
- A set of Initial Beta Content Candidates (Guides, Problem answers, Country orientations) designed as a flexible starting point.
- Clear, honest descriptions of potential local assistance capabilities (marked with availability limitations).
- A simple contact method (e.g., mailto link or basic form) driving users to discuss their requirements.
- AI-readable markdown/HTML structure.
- Multilingual pages *only* where explicitly justified by the Search Intent Map.

## What the Beta WILL NOT Contain
The beta website absolutely will not include:
- User accounts or profiles.
- Login or authentication systems.
- Payment gateways or shopping carts.
- Booking calendars or automated scheduling.
- Marketplaces or supplier databases.
- Interactive dashboards.
- Customer or provider portals.
- Automated service workflows.
