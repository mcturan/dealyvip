# Sprint 2.5 Audit Report

## Executive Summary
Sprint 2.5 successfully defined the content architecture and discovery strategy for the DealyVIP beta website. The content model strictly follows the Information → Trust → Contact funnel. It targets 26 high-value Tier 1 pages focused primarily on Türkiye and Ukraine, avoiding artificial symmetry with heavily sanctioned markets (Russia/Iran) where only compliance overviews are planned. The scope remains strictly informational, entirely rejecting portals, payments, and automated workflows.

## Audit Criteria

### Thin Content Risk
- **Passed:** The `19_SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md` limits the beta launch (Tier 1) to 26 distinct, high-intent pages. There is no mass-generation of generic keyword pages.

### Duplicate Intent & Keyword Cannibalization
- **Passed:** Questions are grouped logically. Canonical pages are prioritized over slight variations.

### Artificial Country Symmetry
- **Passed:** Türkiye and Ukraine have supplier search, local assistance, and verification pages. Russia and Iran are explicitly limited to Trade Restrictions & Sanctions Overviews due to regulatory blocks.

### Unsupported Service Claims & Regulatory Overreach
- **Passed:** `23_LOCAL_ASSISTANCE_CONTENT_MODEL.md` mandates strict availability limitations. No guarantees, legal advice, or official audits are promised.

### AI Discoverability Myths
- **Passed:** `24_AI_DISCOVERABILITY.md` focuses on direct answers, semantic structure, entity clarity, and dates, explicitly avoiding keyword stuffing.

### Overengineering
- **Passed:** `26_BETA_WEBSITE_SCOPE.md` explicitly bans user accounts, logins, payments, booking calendars, and customer portals.

### Contradictions with Sprint 1 and 2
- **Passed:** An initial minor contradiction introducing "Discovery" to the customer funnel was removed. The funnel correctly remains "Information → Trust → Contact".

### Missing High-Value Questions
- **Passed:** Logistics and customs coordination orientation for Türkiye (T1-26) was added after independent review identified it as a missing high-value hurdle.

## Open Decisions
- None required at this architectural stage.

## Final Status
PASS
