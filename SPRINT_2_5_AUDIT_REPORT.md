# Sprint 2.5 Audit Report (Corrected)

## Executive Summary
Sprint 2.5 successfully defined the content architecture and discovery strategy for the DealyVIP beta website. The content model strictly follows the Information → Trust → Contact funnel. It targets high-value initial beta content candidates focused primarily on Türkiye and Ukraine, while structurally allowing case-specific compliance and orientation content for heavily restricted markets (Russia/Iran) without unsupported blanket prohibitions. The scope remains strictly informational, entirely rejecting portals, payments, and automated workflows.

## Audit Criteria

### Thin Content Risk
- **Passed:** The `19_SEARCH_INTENT_MAP.md` limits the beta launch to a focused set of Initial Beta Content Candidates. There is no mass-generation of generic keyword pages.

### Duplicate Intent & Keyword Cannibalization
- **Passed:** Questions are grouped logically. Canonical pages are prioritized over slight variations.

### Country Content Strategy (Corrected)
- **Passed:** A previous overgeneralization implying complete prohibition of trade and banking for Russia and Iran was identified and corrected. The strategy now correctly reflects a case-specific model (dependent on parties, sectors, and jurisdictions) and includes compliance/exemption orientation pages. No claim is made that restricted markets are automatically available, and availability remains case-specific.

### Unsupported Service Claims & Regulatory Overreach
- **Passed:** `23_LOCAL_ASSISTANCE_CONTENT_MODEL.md` mandates strict availability limitations. No guarantees, legal advice, or official audits are promised.

### AI Discoverability Myths
- **Passed:** `24_AI_DISCOVERABILITY.md` focuses on direct answers, semantic structure, entity clarity, and dates, explicitly avoiding keyword stuffing.

### Overengineering
- **Passed:** `26_BETA_WEBSITE_SCOPE.md` explicitly bans user accounts, logins, payments, booking calendars, and customer portals.

### Language Authority (Corrected)
- **Passed:** The previous assumption of strict English supremacy was corrected. The strategy now establishes that each language version must be maintained as a controlled content version, allowing Turkish, Ukrainian, or Russian pages to be authored as original primary pages where justified by search intent or legal context, prioritizing semantic consistency over mass translation.

## Final Status
PASS WITH CORRECTIONS
