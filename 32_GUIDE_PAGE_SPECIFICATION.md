# Guide Page Specification

## Purpose
Define the standard template for informational guides to ensure high AI discoverability and user utility.

## Recommended Structure

1. **Question / Title:** Clear, search-intent driven `H1` (e.g., "How to Verify a Company in Türkiye").
2. **Direct Answer:** A 1-2 sentence bold summary answering the question immediately.
3. **Summary:** Brief context on the importance of the topic.
4. **Key Steps:** Numbered, actionable process instructions.
5. **Important Considerations:** Contextual nuances (e.g., local holidays, unwritten rules).
6. **What Can Be Verified:** Honest capabilities.
7. **What Cannot Be Verified:** Honest limitations to prevent liability.
8. **Risks / Limitations:** Common pitfalls or regulatory constraints.
9. **When Local Assistance May Help (Optional):** Soft pivot to DealyVIP's coordination services.
10. **Related Guides:** Internal links.
11. **Sources (Optional):** Links to official government/registry sites if cited.
12. **Last Updated:** Prominent date stamp.
13. **Editorial Responsibility:** "Published by DealyVIP Research."
14. **Contact CTA:** Relevant inquiry prompt.
