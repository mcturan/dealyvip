# Türkiye–Ukraine Assistance Research

## 1. Practical User Problems
- **Language Barrier:** Cross-border communication often suffers when neither party speaks fluent English.
- **Logistical Complexity:** Due to airspace closures in Ukraine, businessmen traveling from Türkiye to Ukraine face multi-day land journeys requiring complex coordination (flights to Poland/Moldova, trains/buses across the border).
- **Trust Deficit:** Due to regional volatility, suppliers in both countries often demand prepayments, making buyers anxious. Physical presence via local proxies is highly demanded.

## 2. Operational Boundaries (What DealyVIP Can Do)
- **WHAT USERS NEED:** "I need someone to go to this factory in Istanbul and make sure they actually exist and are manufacturing the goods before I send money from Kyiv."
- **WHAT CAN BE EXPLAINED:** We can outline the steps for requesting a capacity report or verifying registry existence.
- **WHAT MAY REQUIRE LOCAL COORDINATION:** Coordinating an independent professional (e.g., a bilingual assistant or inspector) to visit the factory, take photos, and hold a video call with the buyer.
- **WHAT REQUIRES A LICENSED PROFESSIONAL:** Drafting the international sales contract, performing accredited QC (ISO/SGS standards), or executing customs brokerage.

## 3. Disclaimers and Limitations
- DealyVIP coordinates these services but does not directly employ the on-the-ground agents.
- Availability is completely dependent on regional security (especially in Ukraine) and operational capacity at the time of the request.
