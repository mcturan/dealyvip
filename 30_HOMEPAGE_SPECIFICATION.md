# Homepage Specification

## Purpose
Define the structure of the homepage to immediately orient the user, establish the target geography, and funnel them toward specific informational solutions.

## Section 1: Hero
- **Primary message:** Clear statement of value. (e.g., "Navigate Cross-Border Business with Confidence").
- **Secondary message:** Establish the geography and core focus (e.g., "Independent verification, supplier discovery, and local assistance in Türkiye, Ukraine, Russia, and Iran.").
- **Primary CTA:** "Find Local Assistance" (Leads to Assistance hub/Contact).
- **Secondary CTA:** "Read Business Guides" (Leads to Guides hub).

## Section 2: "Explore by Need"
A grid or list highlighting primary user intents:
- Verify a Company
- Find a Supplier
- Plan a Business Visit
- Need an Interpreter

## Section 3: "Explore by Country"
Cards linking to the country hub pages:
- **Türkiye:** Market Entry, Suppliers, Logistics.
- **Ukraine:** Operations under constraint, Verification.
- **Russia:** Compliance, Sanctions, Exemptions.
- **Iran:** Compliance, Trade Restrictions.

## Section 4: Featured Guides
Highlighting 3-4 top Tier 1 guides to demonstrate immediate value (e.g., "Pre-Payment Supplier Checklist").

## Section 5: Why DealyVIP / Editorial Principles
A short trust-building block emphasizing DealyVIP's boundaries:
- Independent information.
- No hidden supplier fees.
- Strict regulatory awareness.

## Section 6: Contact CTA
A bold, bottom-of-page block: "Need tailored local assistance? Tell us your requirements." -> Links to `/contact`.

## Section 7: Footer
Standard footer navigation as defined in the Navigation Model.
